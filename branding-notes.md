# Government Identity Notes

The Tamil Nadu Electricity Board public site confirms that its previous `.gov.in` address has migrated to `tnebltd.org` and displays the established circular electricity-board mark. The command-centre masthead will use a restrained Government of Tamil Nadu emblem and Tamil Nadu Electricity Board mark, with accessible text labels retained alongside each visual mark.

The government emblem and electricity-board visual marks will be treated as institutional identifiers only; the product name remains **Transformer Health Command**.

For the prototype city map, Coimbatore is centred near 11.0168 N, 76.9558 E, using the public coordinate reference surfaced at https://www.mapsofworld.com/lat_long/coimbatore.html. The expanded Coimbatore zone boundaries and transformer markers remain visual prototype data until operational GIS records are onboarded.

Direct retrieval of official image files from `tn.gov.in` and the migrated electricity-board website was unavailable during this update. The current marks are visual placeholders that match the public institutional identity and should be replaced with administrator-provided or directly downloaded official source files before production publication.
